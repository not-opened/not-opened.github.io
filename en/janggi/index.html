Your first Janggi moves

Janggi (장기) is Korean chess. Pieces sit on the intersections of a 9 × 10 board. Start with four moves, one piece at a time.

Four pieces at a glance

  • Chariot (차): Moves in a straight line if the path is clear. It can also follow a marked palace diagonal.
  • Horse (마): One point straight, then one point diagonally outward. If the first point is occupied, it cannot make that move.
  • Cannon (포): It must jump exactly one non-cannon piece to move or capture. It cannot capture another cannon.
  • General (궁): One point along a marked line in its own palace. It cannot move into check.

Read the full movement rules at Janggi.gg. These are practice positions, not a full match.
