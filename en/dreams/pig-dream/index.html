Korean folklore

Pig dreams in Korean folklore

Why a pig can suggest abundance in Korean storytelling.

What a pig dream means in one tradition

돼지꿈 (dwaeji-kkum) simply means “pig dream.” The National Museum of Korea describes an association between pig dreams and wealth in folk tradition, and notes that pigs also symbolized fertility. In that context, it can be called a 길몽 (gilmong), a dream traditionally regarded as auspicious.

A symbol is not a forecast

Hearing “that sounds lucky” can be a way of sharing someone’s delight in an unusual dream. It does not tell you that money is coming, and it gives no reason to gamble, spend, or change financial plans. People can recognize the reference without taking it literally.

Try a different meaning of abundance

Think about what “having enough” would mean to you this week. It could be time to cook, a quieter morning, or a meal with someone you miss. If the dream felt funny instead of lucky, keep that detail. The feeling you noticed belongs to your telling of the dream; the folklore gives you another story to compare it with.

One question to keep

Did the pig feel like a welcome guest, a nuisance, or simply part of the scene?
