Dream reflection

Falling

A missed stair, the ground giving way, or a drop that seems to go on forever.

Find the moment the scene changes

The instant before a fall can be more memorable than the drop itself. If you were reaching for a rail, the question might be what you expected to hold. If you were already falling in the first scene, there may be no missing step to reconstruct. Sometimes a few words about the sensation are all you want to keep.

A fall in a Korean folktale

The National Folk Museum of Korea’s entry on the catfish’s dream tells of a fish that moves between extraordinary places, including a sudden plunge. Two characters interpret the story very differently. Their disagreement is part of the folktale’s humor, not a fixed rule for what falling in a dream predicts.

Keep the dream separate from a decision

Try writing a plain description first: “I stepped down and the stair wasn’t there.” Then write one word for the feeling. If that reminds you of a situation in which you wanted steadier footing, consider what information or help would be useful right now. If it doesn’t remind you of anything, there is no puzzle you have to solve.

One question to keep

Did the ground give way, did your foot slip, or did the dream begin in midair?
