Dream reflection

Flying

Gliding close to the ground, soaring high, or struggling to steer.

Two kinds of freedom

Floating just above the pavement might feel playful; looking down from far above it might feel lonely or exposed. Choosing your own route is another detail entirely. Before reaching for a meaning, ask what flying let you do in that particular dream: see farther, cross something, leave a place, or simply have fun.

The dragon is its own motif

The National Folk Museum of Korea’s dictionary entry on dragon dreams records a motif involving riding a dragon or seeing one rise, traditionally associated with rising in social rank. A dream about flying without a dragon is a different scene. Even if a dragon appeared, the belief is cultural history, not a promise of promotion or success.

Follow the detail you remember

If the flight felt good, write down the freedom you enjoyed: taking your time, looking around, or choosing a direction. If it felt scary, ask whether it was the height, the speed, or the difficulty of landing. You can appreciate a vivid scene without turning it into a plan or an omen.

One question to keep

Could you choose where to go, or did the wind decide?
